Specific Resolution (ECE-MP1 Coherence): The deck cites ECE12-13 with band-integrated (80-100 kHz) coherence (~0.06). The fresh run confirms this band-integrated value (0.056 for ECE12, 0.064 for ECE13). However, evaluating single-bin coherence exactly at 89 kHz reveals that ECE03FAST is the peak channel (gamma^2 = 0.187). Because the mode is narrowband, band-integration artificially dilutes the coherence with noise. The deck should be updated to cite the single-bin metric for ECE03FAST.
