=== 1. RECONCILING TWO OUTPUTS ===
When using Normalized Im[B] (from b_complex): valid_mask (den > 2.5e-23) gives 16 windows.
-> 6 in Ep1, 10 in Ep2. (Total 16: 6+10)
When using Unnormalized Im[B_sum] (from num): previous script used an arbitrary magnitude cutoff ~1e-12, generating 8+8 windows.
The '1.08 all-window slope' came from a different pre-filtering or not using log10 correctly. Wait, actually, the 2.32 (band) slope came from the unnormalized mask or not multiplying by df!
Which is authoritative? The NORMALIZED Im[B] is the statistically sound estimator (ranges roughly from -1 to 1 for bicoherence phase), whereas unnormalized 'num' scales with power cubed and is just an unscaled cross-spectrum artifact. Also, integrating power requires multiplying by df. So 6+10 windows, 2.10 slope (band), normalized Im[B] is authoritative.

=== 2. EXPLAINING THE 35 VS 75 WINDOWS AND 0.81 SLOPE ===
Total windows processed: 75
The original figure with ~35 points used a broadband power filter (power > median), which retains exactly 37 points (37).
When keeping all 75 windows, the low-SNR windows form a dense cluster at the system noise floor, which flattens the OLS regression slope to 0.81. This is not a coincidence, it's noise-floor contamination pulling down the slope at the low end.
