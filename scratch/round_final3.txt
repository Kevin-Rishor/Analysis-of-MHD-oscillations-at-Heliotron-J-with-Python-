3. Conclusion on Phase Slips vs. Triad Hopping:
The massive drop in long-window b2 (from ~0.066 to ~0.0004) is heavily explained by triad hopping (moving frequencies). The peak triad drifts out of the 2-bin tolerance for almost 60% of the windows throughout the 41.5 ms duration. Because the interacting frequencies are not constant, phase mixing destructively interferes over the long integration, suppressing the long-window b2 value.
