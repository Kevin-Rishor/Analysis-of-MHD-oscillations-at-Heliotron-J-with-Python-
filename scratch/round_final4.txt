2. Radial profile:
a) The 95% noise floor used for ECE-MP1 coherence (259.1-275.0 ms, 89 kHz) with nperseg=2048 and noverlap=1024 is 0.2058.
The ECE9 (0.105), ECE10 (0.045), ECE12 (0.112), and ECE13 (0.1055) coherences all fall below this 95% floor.

b) The discrepancy in RMS values for ECE13 between the table (0.627 V) and the figure (0.48 V) is due to different analysis windows and filter bands:
- The table (from obj1 scripts) uses a band of 80-100 kHz and a strict active window of 259.1-275.0 ms.
- The figure (from comparison scripts) uses a mode-tracking band of 79-99 kHz and spans the full ECH+NBI phase window of 259.1-291.3 ms.
Both use the standard deviation for normalization.

c) RMS for ECE channels:
- ECE10FAST: 0.1105 V (259-275 ms), 0.0016 V (400-410 ms, quiet)
- ECE11FAST: 0.0061 V (259-275 ms), 0.0058 V (400-410 ms, quiet)
- ECE12FAST: 0.3321 V (259-275 ms), 0.0016 V (400-410 ms, quiet)
The 62.5 GHz (ECE11) dip is NOT a real radial node. It is a dead channel / hardware problem. Its signal level is at the absolute noise floor (~0.006 V) across both active and quiet phases, whereas its neighbours show large fluctuations that drop to near zero during the quiet phase.

The circular standard deviation of the biphase over the windows where it is the strongest triad is 86.69 degrees.
Because the biphase angle drifts significantly (circular std > 45 deg), the biphase is NOT stable; phase slips explain the loss of long-term coherence.
